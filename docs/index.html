    How to add medications

    Type a name, choose the correct medication and form from the suggestions, choose a strength when available, then select Add medication. Repeat for each medication, up to 10.

    Strength is the dose (for example, 20 mg). Form is the type (tablet, capsule, liquid, injection, or extended release), shown in the suggestion. TABS means tablets; CAPS means capsules. Confirm unfamiliar abbreviations in the source.

    Combination medications contain multiple drugs. Choose the exact product prescribed. Suggestions come from imported Devoted formularies, not a complete medication catalog. If yours is missing, use Add manually; it remains unverified. A suggestion does not confirm coverage.

    Select the exact provider and office to check. Maximum 3 providers/facilities per search. Searches go to Devoted’s live directory; suggestions do not confirm coverage. Facility participation does not confirm coverage for every clinician there. Phone numbers appear when supplied by the directory.

      What matters most?

      Choose High, Medium, or Low importance. Choose Not a priority to leave a benefit out of ranking. Ranking compares benefit amounts, not overall suitability.

      More priorities

      Medical costs use in-network amounts. Hospital comparison models one 7-day admission. Hearing compares the Advanced option. Read source conditions.

      Prescription costs require exact drug, pharmacy, and coverage verification; they are not scored yet. Medication and provider checks remain review requirements.

      Coverage requires confirmation for the exact plan, medication, pharmacy, and provider location. A search match alone does not confirm coverage. No patient names or identifiers are needed. Provider names or NPIs are sent to Devoted only when you search. Medication searches run locally in your browser.
